Education and healthcare are also fields that constantly fascinate me, I find myself coming back to core questions in these domains constantly (mainly about how to deliver their best outcomes reliably without letting human incompetence or malice get in the way).&#x20;

## Work

I've found that having work feel personal is absolutely non-negotiable for me and that happens when I work hard and care about what I'm doing. As much as I've tried to manage otherwise in the past, these values are what I constantly work towards, and they are sine qua non for me:&#x20;

* Repping the team jersey and feeling a sense of personal ownership and responsibility at whatever I'm doing.
* High competence, agency and kindness for/from peers. No zero-sum mentality when thinking about knowledge sharing, career development and how to best work towards goals.&#x20;
* Working with people whom I look forward to being in the trenches with, people who are not just the best at what they do but are even better human beings with high integrity and character.
* Colleagues who work stupendously hard and emulate (and thus inspire) dignity, respect, competence, radical honesty, kindness and accountability.&#x20;
