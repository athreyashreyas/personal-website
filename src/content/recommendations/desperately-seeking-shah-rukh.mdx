> A delicious concoction of insightful socio-economics, gender studies, Bollywood and the lives of a diverse group of Indian women.

Shrayana Bhattacharya is a brilliant developmental economist who simply adores Shah Rukh Khan. So do millions of other women across India, and it is in asking the "why" to Shah Rukh's popularity do we get to go on a thoroughly informative, entertaining and illuminating ride across the lives of many wonderful women. \
\
The author uses the symbol of a bollywood icon to help understand gender divide, the wants/needs of Indian women and the female gaze. She gives voice to the dreams, aspirations, complaints and thoughts of Indian women by doing justice to their story, their hero all while shedding light on the socio-political reality of women in India. She does it all so masterfully that by the end of the book you're well taught about gender politics in this country, while chock full of love, empathy and kinship.

Read this the next time you get the non-fiction itch and I can promise you that you'll have a ton of fun.
