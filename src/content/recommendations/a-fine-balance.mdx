> Rohinton Mistry's masterpiece. Speaks of India during the emergency and introduces characters from all walks of life whose lives intersect in myriads of ways.&#x20;

I was at a loss for words when I finished this book, having never before felt the power of a book to move you so viscerally as I first did here. I think its best to read this story going in blind without any context of the plot or its themes.&#x20;

&#x20;

Everything that happens in this book is very plausible, it is what must have been the reality of many people during the emergency period in India. Ultimately that is what this piece of literature is about: everybody's unique stories, their history and how the emergency period's rash policies impacted millions of people in India.&#x20;
