> Memoirs of Singapore's first prime minister: Lessons in nation building, governance, diplomacy and effective public policy.&#x20;

This is the finest non-fiction book and memoir I have ever read. Singapore was a swamp smaller than the size of Bangalore city. That the Singapore we know today emerged from the ashes of the Second World War, the collapse of British colonial rule, the turbulence of communist unrest, and a bitter expulsion from Malaysia is nothing short of extraordinary.\
\
No matter which walk of life you are from, reading this will teach you how to embody first principles thinking, good management, administration, patriotism and how you can achieve extraordinary things with sparse resources, great teammates and unyielding will.&#x20;
